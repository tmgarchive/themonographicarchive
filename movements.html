CAT-1: Materiality & Tactile Surface

Temporal Horizon: 1945–Present

Focuses on raw pigment density, organic mineral dust, slashed and built-up canvas, and heavy physical depth. Rejects passive illusionism in favor of concrete material presence.

KEY MASTERS: TÀPIES, BURRI, DUBUFFET, KIEFER

CAT-2: Conceptual Linguistics & Notation

Temporal Horizon: 1960–Present

Documents written codes, serial notation, repetitive temporal records, and linguistic archives. Investigates language and textual units as fundamental art historical structures.

KEY MASTERS: KAWARA, OPAŁKA, DARBOVEN, TWOMBLY

CAT-3: Process & Dragged Abstraction

Temporal Horizon: 1950–Present

Focuses on process-driven mark-making, mechanical squeegee striations, spatial gestures, and the deliberate tension between total control and physical chance.

KEY MASTERS: RICHTER, FONTANA, ROTHKO, KLINE

CAT-4: Global Contemporary Narrative Archives

Temporal Horizon: 1995–Present

Examines participatory story gathering, socio-economic memory, cross-cultural gesture, and multi-institutional linguistic archives translated onto monumental tactile canvas surfaces.

KEY MASTERS: KENTRIDGE, MEHRETU, ANATSUI, MALIK
